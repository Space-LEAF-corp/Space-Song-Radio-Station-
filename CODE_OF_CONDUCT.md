Space Song Radio Station
Purpose

The Space Song Radio Station project is a public demo environment built on transparency, creativity, and remix‑friendly collaboration.
This Code of Conduct ensures that all contributors, listeners, developers, and remixers participate in a safe, respectful, and inclusive space.

Everyone interacting with this repository agrees to follow the standards below.

---

Our Standards

We commit to a community where people treat each other with respect, curiosity, and professionalism.

Positive Behaviors

• Respectful Communication — clear, kind, and constructive dialogue
• Collaborative Creativity — supporting remixers, coders, and artists
• Transparency — honest documentation of contributions, sources, and methods
• Inclusive Participation — welcoming contributors of all backgrounds and skill levels
• Constructive Feedback — focusing on improvement, not personal criticism


Unacceptable Behaviors

• Harassment or Discrimination of any kind
• Hostile or Dismissive Communication
• Uploading Copyrighted or Unlicensed Material
• Misuse of Generative Models
• Sabotaging or Corrupting the Demo Environment
• Misrepresenting Contributions


This project is a public demo — transparency and safety are non‑negotiable.

---

Audio & Generative Content Rules

Because this repository includes demo tracks, stems, and generative outputs, contributors must follow additional guidelines:

• Only Upload Audio You Have Rights To
• Label All Versions Clearly
• Document Generative Processes
• Avoid Harmful or Deceptive Model Outputs
• Respect Attribution Requirements


This ensures the public can remix safely and legally.

---

Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness a violation:

• Open a Conduct Issue
• Provide Clear Evidence
• Request Private Review if needed


Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

---

Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

• Warnings
• Temporary or Permanent Bans
• Rejection of Contributions
• Removal of Harmful Content


Maintainers must document enforcement actions for transparency.

---

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

• All repository activity
• Pull requests and issues
• Audio uploads and remix submissions
• Discussions, comments, and community spaces
• Any interaction representing the project


---

Commitment to Growth

The Space Song Radio Station is an evolving ecosystem.
As new generative engines, models, and audio systems are added, this Code of Conduct will expand to ensure safety, transparency, and creative freedom remain at the core.

---

Thank You

By participating in this project, you help build a respectful, open, remix‑friendly creative environment.
Your contributions — musical, technical, or conceptual — help shape the future of this station.
